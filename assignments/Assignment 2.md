# Assignment 2: Midterm oral exam

{: .due }
**Due: Sign up for slot between Nov 9–17. Booking link TBD.** 

This midterm oral exam is intended to be a collegial conversation between the instructor and student about the course themes and readings. At least 24 hours before the exam, you will submit a **1 page handout** online (please also bring a hard copy to the exam). This handout should include:

1. A verbatim short passage (<500 words) from one of the class readings that you found confusing, ambiguous, provocative, or otherwise questionable (be prepared to articulate why) with 2 sentences linking this passage to _another_ reading from the class.
2. A list of 5 class readings ranked by some axis of your choosing (_not_ publication date, authors in alphabetical order, etc). Write one sentence per reading justifying its position relative to the others. In one sentence, identify one adjacent-ranking pair that you might expect me to ask about and preview your reasoning.

This is an open-book, analog exam: you can bring hard copies of your class notes, readings, previous assignments, etc. and refer to them at any time.
