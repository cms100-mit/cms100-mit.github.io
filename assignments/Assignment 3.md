# Assignment 3: Media analysis project

Add a slide to the shared deck with no more than 3 short ideas on what you would like to research for your final project (1–3 sentences per idea). Be prepared to speak for ~3 minutes and answer questions.

## (A3.2) Project proposal and annotated bibliography (1000 words)

{: .due }
**Due: 9 am on Oct 23 → submit to [Canvas](https://canvas.mit.edu/courses/40342).**

**Project proposal (600 words)**
* _Introduce the object of study (phenomenon, controversy, system, etc)._ What is it? Why does it matter? Why should you (or we) care?
* _State the research question:_ what is the fundamental thing you are trying to learn by pursuing it?
* _Outline plan to complete project:_ how will you break up the project into manageable chunks? What kinds of research will you need to do? How will you get it done in time? What kind of assistance will you need?

**Annotated bibliography (400 words):** Select at least 7 sources that you will use in the final project and write a few bullet points or sentences about each source and how they interact with the others. Write one synthesis paragraph at the top about what you found, what you still need, and what your gaps are.

## (A3.6) Two peer review memos (written, 350 words each)

{: .due }
**Due: 9 am, Nov 25 → Submit to [Canvas](https://canvas.mit.edu/courses/40342).**

You will be assigned 2 different colleagues’ work to evaluate. After reading their project proposal, annotated bibliography, and first draft, and oral presentation, write a memo for each person with the following components: (1) what is the project’s core argument? (2) What were its major strengths and weaknesses? (3) Name at least 2 tangible ways for the author to improve their paper / project. **You may not upload your colleagues’ work into any AI model of any kind.**
