# Calendar

{: .important }
**The syllabus is a living document and is liable to change.** You will never get more reading than is listed here; readings may be swapped for something of equivalent length or less. There should be no more than ~30 pages of reading per class session, or around 1–1.5 hours of reading.

Readings hosted on `libproxy.mit.edu` require you to be signed in with your MIT certificates or Touchstone. **Bring hard copies of the readings to class** — you will need them for reading responses and discussion. Registrar deadlines and holidays are marked in the week where they fall.
